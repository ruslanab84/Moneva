## Smart Insights

[SmartInsights.swift](Core/SmartInsights.swift) computes spending-trend/spike/budget-projection signals with pure `Decimal` statistics off the main actor; Foundation Models is only allowed to pick which of two app-authored explanation strings to show (a constrained choice index), never to write numbers. See [../../docs/smart-insights/README.md](../../docs/smart-insights/README.md) for the exact thresholds.
